My Favorite Places in Radnor Township

Radnor Township is a great place to visit! There are great trails, resturants, and it is a very quaint area in the mainline.

Capri

A small family-owned water ice shop. This was walking distance from my middle school, so almost every Friday, my friends and I would walk here and get a sweet treat after school. Fun fact, my family friend is part owner!

Osushi

Probably the best sushi place in the township. I go to a LOT of sushi places, and for this town, it has great quality sushi.

Willows Park

Willows Park has great nature trails and fishing ponds. I walk this trail with my friend and her dog, who is unironically named Willow!

DanDan

Not my favorite place in the world, but I work here! It serves Sichuan and Taiwanese food, and I have been hosting here for three years.
